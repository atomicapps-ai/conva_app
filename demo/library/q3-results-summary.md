# Q3 2026 results — summary for leadership

## Headline numbers

- **Revenue:** $11.8 million, up 22% on Q3 2025 and 6% on Q2.
- **Net revenue retention:** 117%.
- **New customers:** 38, against a plan of 35.
- **Gross margin:** 78%, down from 80% because of higher hosting costs after the offline-sync launch.
- **Cash:** $19.4 million at quarter end; runway about 31 months at current burn.

## What went well

- The Business plan now makes up 61% of new bookings.
- Churn fell to 1.4% a quarter from 2.1%.
- Support first-response time for severity 1 improved to 38 minutes.

## What did not

- Enterprise sales cycles lengthened to 118 days from 94.
- Two integrations (Cedarline Logistics and one payroll connector) caused 11 support escalations.
- Hiring for the design team is two people behind plan.
