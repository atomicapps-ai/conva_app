# Master services terms — Larkspur Foods (summary)

This is a plain-language summary for the account team. The signed agreement governs.

## Term and renewal

- Initial term ran 1 December 2024 to 30 November 2025, then renewed for one year.
- Either party must give **60 days' written notice** before the renewal date to change or end the agreement.
- Renewal price increases are capped at **7% per year** unless seats or plan change.

## Service levels

- Availability commitment: **99.9% monthly**, excluding scheduled maintenance.
- Credits: 5% of the monthly fee for each full 0.1% below the commitment, up to 25%.
- Priority support response: within 1 hour for severity 1, 4 hours for severity 2.

## Data and security

- Customer data is stored in the United States.
- Audit logs are kept for 90 days on Business and 400 days with the Audit Pack.
- Larkspur may export all data in standard formats at any time at no charge.

## Seats

- Seats can be added mid-term at the contracted rate, prorated.
- Seats cannot be reduced below the committed count until renewal.
