# Battle card — Quill Analytics

Quill Analytics sells a reporting-first operations tool. It is the competitor Larkspur's procurement team approached for a comparison quote.

## Where Quill is strong

- Lower headline price: **$31 per seat per month** on a three-year commitment.
- Polished dashboards for executives.

## Where Brightwater wins

- **Shift handoffs:** Quill has no structured handoff workflow; notes are free text. Brightwater Flow captures handoffs as tracked tasks with owners.
- **Offline mode:** Brightwater works in warehouse dead zones and syncs later. Quill needs a live connection.
- **Switching cost:** moving 300 users means retraining three shifts and rebuilding every handoff template; Larkspur's own pilot estimate was 10 weeks.
- **Support:** Brightwater's severity 1 response is 1 hour; Quill's published target is next business day.

## Handling the price objection

Do not match the price. Compare the total cost: Quill's three-year lock-in, extra fees for offline use and paid migration, and the estimated cost of the longer rollout. Offer the volume discount on the new sites instead.
