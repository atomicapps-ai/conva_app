# Take-home design exercise (3 hours)

## Scenario

A night-shift supervisor is handing a warehouse zone to the morning supervisor. Nine tasks are open; two are blocked waiting for a part; the tablet loses connection for 20 minutes during the handoff.

## Your task

Design the handoff screen on a tablet. Show:

1. How open and blocked tasks are summarised.
2. How the outgoing supervisor records a note and assigns an owner.
3. What the app shows while offline and after it reconnects.

## Deliverable

A short walkthrough (five slides or a prototype link) and a one-page rationale. Do not spend more than three hours.

## How we will review it

We care about your reasoning and trade-offs more than polish. See the interview rubric for the scoring dimensions.
