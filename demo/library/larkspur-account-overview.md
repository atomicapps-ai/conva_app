# Larkspur Foods — account overview

Larkspur Foods is a regional grocery distributor serving about 210 stores across the Pacific Northwest. It has roughly 1,400 employees and has been a Brightwater Labs customer since March 2024.

## Relationship at a glance

- **Plan:** Business, 300 seats at $42 per seat per month, billed annually ($151,200 a year).
- **Renewal date:** 30 November 2026. The master services terms require 60 days' written notice to change or cancel, so the practical deadline is 1 October.
- **Executive sponsor:** Priya Raman, VP Operations.
- **Procurement lead:** Tomás Becker.
- **Day-to-day owner:** Jun Park, Warehouse Systems Manager.
- **Brightwater owner:** the account executive on this call.

## Why they bought

Larkspur's warehouses hand work between three shifts a day. Before Brightwater Flow, handoff notes lived in spreadsheets and radio calls, and about 6% of orders were mis-picked because a note was missed. Priya's stated goal for year two was to halve that rate.

## What changed in 2026

- Two new distribution centres opened (Spokane and Boise), adding about 120 warehouse staff.
- Procurement now requires a competing quote for any contract over $100,000.
- Quill Analytics has pitched Larkspur at $31 per seat per month.

## Open questions before renewal

1. Does Larkspur want to expand to the two new sites (about 120 more seats)?
2. Will they accept the proposed Audit Pack add-on ($18,000 a year)?
3. Can the price increase stay within the 7% cap in the current terms?
