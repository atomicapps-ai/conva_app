# Launch FAQ

**When is 3.0 available?** 20 October for all plans.

**Does 3.0 cost more?** No. Team stays at $24 per seat per month and Business at $42. The Audit Pack is an add-on at $1,500 a month.

**Does it work offline?** Yes. Offline mode 2.0 queues changes and shows conflicts when you reconnect.

**Can we migrate handoff notes from spreadsheets?** Yes, with the import tool; most sites finish in a day.

**What is the availability commitment?** 99.9% monthly on all paid plans.

**Where is data stored?** In the United States.
