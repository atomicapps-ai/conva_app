# Senior Product Designer — Brightwater Labs

**Team:** Product Design (6 people) · **Location:** Portland or remote (US time zones) · **Reports to:** Head of Design

## The role

You will own the design of Brightwater Flow's handoff and task-tracking experience, used daily by warehouse and operations teams on tablets and phones, often in poor connectivity.

## What you will do

- Lead design from discovery through launch for one product area at a time.
- Run research visits at customer sites, including warehouses.
- Design for offline-first behaviour: queued actions, sync states, conflict resolution.
- Partner with two engineers and one product manager in a squad.
- Contribute to the design system, which has 140 components.

## What we look for

- Six or more years designing shipped B2B or operational software.
- A portfolio showing problem framing, not only visuals.
- Experience with accessibility on touch devices (large targets, glare, gloves).
- Comfort with data: you can read usage dashboards and propose experiments.

## Compensation

Base salary range $148,000 to $176,000, plus equity and a learning budget of $2,500 a year.

## Process

Recruiter screen, hiring-manager conversation, take-home design exercise (three hours), panel with design, product and engineering, then references.
