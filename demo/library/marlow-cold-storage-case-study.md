# Case study — Marlow Cold Storage

Marlow Cold Storage runs four temperature-controlled warehouses and adopted Brightwater Flow in 2025.

## Results after 90 days

- Shift handoff errors fell **41%**.
- Average handoff time dropped from 14 minutes to 6 minutes.
- Mis-picked orders fell from 5.2% to 3.1%.
- Training time for new hires fell from five days to three.

## What they did

1. Replaced paper handoff sheets with a Brightwater handoff template per dock.
2. Gave every shift lead a tablet running offline mode.
3. Reviewed the weekly handoff report in the Monday operations meeting.

## Quote

Marlow's operations director said the biggest change was that nobody argued about what had been handed over, because it was all on the record.
