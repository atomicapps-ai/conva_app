# Briefing — Larkspur Foods renewal

**Goal:** renew the 300 existing seats and win the 120-seat expansion before procurement's competing-quote review closes.

## Key facts

- Current spend $151,200 a year; renewal 30 November 2026; 60-day notice window.
- Mis-pick rate improved from 5.8% to 3.4% since January; Priya's target is roughly 3%.
- Quill Analytics quoted $31 per seat on a three-year term.
- Expansion proposal: 120 seats at a 12% discount plus a $4,200 onboarding fee; Audit Pack $18,000 a year.

## Say-now lines

- "Your mis-pick rate is down from 5.8% to 3.4% in nine months, which is the number Priya set out to move."
- "Quill's price looks lower, but their three-year lock-in and offline fees change the comparison."

## Watch for

Any move toward a three-year term from procurement; any request to reduce seats below 300 (not allowed before renewal).
