# Larkspur renewal — prepared questions and answers

**Q: Why should we pay more than Quill's quote?**
A: Compare the full cost, not the seat price. Quill requires a three-year commitment, charges extra for offline use and migration, and has no structured handoff workflow, which is the reason Larkspur bought Brightwater.

**Q: What will renewal cost for our current 300 seats?**
A: The same $151,200 a year at current pricing. Any increase is capped at 7% under the master services terms.

**Q: What does the expansion to Spokane and Boise cost?**
A: 120 seats at $42 a month for 12 months with a 12% volume discount comes to $53,222 a year, plus a one-time onboarding fee of $4,200.

**Q: What is the Audit Pack?**
A: An add-on that extends audit log retention from 90 to 400 days and adds exportable audit reports. It is $1,500 a month, $18,000 a year.

**Q: How much has the mis-pick rate improved?**
A: From 5.8% in January to 3.4% in September 2026, against Priya's goal of halving the rate.

**Q: What is the deadline to change the contract?**
A: 60 days before 30 November, which is 1 October.
