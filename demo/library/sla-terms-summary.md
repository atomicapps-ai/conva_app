# Service levels — summary

## Brightwater availability

- **99.9% monthly** on all paid plans.
- A severity 1 incident that causes more than 43 minutes of unavailability in a month triggers service credits.
- Credits are 5% of the monthly fee for each full 0.1% below the commitment, up to 25%.

## Third-party integrations

- Integration partners are **excluded** from the availability commitment, but Brightwater commits to notify affected customers within 3 hours of detecting an integration incident.
- A partner outage that Brightwater could have mitigated within its own systems counts toward the commitment.

## Response times

| Severity | First response | Update cadence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (outage) | 1 hour | every 30 minutes |
| 2 (degraded) | 4 hours | every 4 hours |
| 3 (question) | 1 business day | on change |
