## [0.7.0]

Conva's Live workspace is more capable and easier to control: Ally can work with domain terminology and spreadsheet figures, while clearer privacy settings let you decide what the app may send or retain.

### Highlights

- **Live workspace:** the Ally panel now separates Active findings from View, which can open in its own attached window. Context documents stay scoped to the selected Context, and highlighted terms resolve against the domain lexicon with better phrase matching.
- **Structured answers:** Ally can calculate exact spreadsheet totals progressively and link them to their source cells.
- **First run and model choice:** choose how Ally should think during setup, compare supported models by measured speed and quality, and select quality and fast models with clearer cost guidance.
- **Web Contexts:** the browser setup wizard can now create a Context, attach its Library documents, and finish setup successfully.

### Privacy and control

- Turn off calls to hosted AI providers with the new offline mode; local Ollama remains available.
- Review and remove local recordings or other local data, erase local app data, and request account deletion from Profile.
- Manage usage telemetry in Settings. Accounts covered by beta terms see the setting locked on, with the reason learned at sign-in.
- The app explains when content leaves the computer and adds an answer-check reminder, first-run key guidance, and bundled open-source licence notices.

### Reliability

- Long transcripts use a windowed view to prevent memory growth; idle Live sessions can stop automatically, and model, transcription, and account errors are clearer.
- The first run rehearsal now creates its Library documents and Context through the browser UI and verifies that download preserves the original file.
- Fixed the Distil-small English model download, improved term and speaker presentation, and refined Context, Library, setup, and account flows.
- Updated vulnerable dependencies and added secret and dependency scanning to CI.
